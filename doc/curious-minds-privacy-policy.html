Curious Minds is designed for children aged 5–14. We take privacy seriously. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data — not even names, email addresses, or device identifiers.

📋

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how The Young Legends ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Curious Minds ("the App"). The App is designed for children aged 5–14 and is made available on the Google Play Store (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS).

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you are a parent or guardian, please read this policy carefully before allowing your child to use the App.

🚫

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users of this App. Specifically, we do not collect:

Names Email addresses Phone numbers Device identifiers IP addresses Location data Usage analytics Crash reports Photos or camera data Contacts
📱

Local Device Storage

The App saves certain non-personal information locally on the user's device only. This data never leaves the device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. This local data includes:

Quiz scores XP points Earned badges Think Fast scores App preferences

This data exists solely to provide a personalised in-app experience and is fully controlled by the user. It can be erased at any time by uninstalling the App.

🌐

Internet & Third-Party Services

Curious Minds is a fully offline app. It does not require an internet connection to function and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or cloud services during normal use.

We do not use any of the following in this App:

Google Analytics Firebase Facebook SDK Advertising networks Crash reporting tools Social media integrations
👶

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Curious Minds is specifically designed for children aged 5 to 14. We are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable data protection laws in India and internationally.

Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, there is nothing to disclose, share, or delete. No account creation is required. No parental consent is needed beyond allowing the child to use the App.

Parents and guardians can contact us at any time with questions about their child's use of the App.

📢

Advertisements

This App does not display any advertisements. There are no banner ads, interstitial ads, video ads, or in-app purchases. The App is a clean, ad-free learning experience for children.

We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
